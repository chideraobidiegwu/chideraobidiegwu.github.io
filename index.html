Supply Chain Performance Review — "When Late Means Loss"

Analyzed DataCo's global shipping performance to uncover why 75% of orders ship late — and what it's costing the business.

  • Late orders brought in ~₦1.11 less profit on average than on-time orders

Analyzed 101,766 hospital encounters to pinpoint the true drivers of a 46.1% readmission rate, translating findings into recommendations hospital operations could act on.
